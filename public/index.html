Questions Before You Order?

Here are answers to some of the questions customers ask before choosing a NexProxy plan.

What is a USA mobile proxy?

A USA mobile proxy routes your internet traffic through a mobile network connection in the United States, giving your connection a USA mobile IP.

Are the proxies dedicated?

NexProxy provides dedicated proxy access for customers. Proxy availability and network characteristics may vary depending on the assigned proxy.

Which carriers are available?

Our USA mobile proxy inventory may include AT&T, Verizon, and T-Mobile networks. Carrier availability depends on current inventory.

Can I choose a USA state?

Yes, state selection may be available when the requested location is in stock. If a specific state is unavailable, we may assign another available USA location.

Do you offer 4G and 5G proxies?

Our inventory includes both 4G and 5G mobile proxies. Availability depends on the proxy currently in stock. Both types may be offered under the same package pricing.

What happens if my proxy stops working?

If your assigned proxy becomes unavailable or stops working, contact our support team. We will provide a replacement proxy when the issue is confirmed.

Do you offer refunds?

Purchases are non-refundable. If there is a problem with your proxy, please contact support so we can provide a replacement when applicable.

How does the free trial work?

New customers can start the 10-minute Free Trial for $0. No payment is required for the trial. After the trial, choose Starter, Growth, Professional, Business, or a Custom quantity from 1 to 100 proxies.

Monthly plan pricing, discounts, and custom savings are shown before an order is submitted.
