  • Prevent abuse or misuse of our services
  • Improve the website and service

We do not use customer information for purposes unrelated to providing or operating the service unless permitted or required by applicable law.

If you contact NexProxy through email, Telegram, or another supported communication channel, we may retain relevant communication information so that we can:
