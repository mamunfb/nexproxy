Refund & Replacement Policy

Last updated: August 21, 2026

1. Overview

This Refund & Replacement Policy explains how NexProxy handles refund requests, trial purchases, monthly purchases, and proxy replacement requests.

We aim to provide reliable service and reasonable support when a purchased proxy cannot be delivered or experiences a verified service failure.

This policy should be read together with our Terms of Service and Acceptable Use Policy .

2. Refund Policy

Purchases are not automatically refundable simply because a customer changes their mind or no longer needs the service.

A refund request may be considered when the purchased service cannot be delivered, a material service issue cannot reasonably be resolved, or a refund is required by applicable law or the rules of the payment provider used for the transaction.

Refund decisions are evaluated based on the specific circumstances of the order and the nature of the reported issue.

3. Trial Purchases

The NexProxy Free Trial is priced at $0 for 10 minutes.

Trial purchases are generally non-refundable after the service has been delivered and access has been provided.

If a trial proxy cannot be delivered or has a verified service failure that cannot reasonably be resolved, please contact support so the issue can be reviewed.

4. Monthly Purchases

NexProxy monthly plans are priced according to the selected Starter, Growth, Professional, Business, or Custom quantity offer shown during ordering.

Monthly purchases are generally non-refundable after the service has been delivered and access has been provided.

If a proxy experiences a verified service failure, NexProxy may provide a replacement in accordance with the circumstances of the issue.

5. Proxy Replacement

NexProxy may provide a replacement when a purchased proxy is verified to have a service-related failure.

Replacement support is intended to resolve genuine service problems and is not intended to cover issues caused by customer configuration, incompatible software, third-party restrictions, or prohibited use.

Replacement requests are reviewed individually based on the reported problem and available verification.

6. What Counts as Service Failure

A service failure may include circumstances where a delivered proxy cannot reasonably provide the purchased service due to a verified problem with the proxy itself.

Examples may include a proxy that cannot establish a connection or a proxy that is otherwise confirmed to be unavailable for the purchased service.

NexProxy may request technical information or testing results to verify the reported issue before providing a replacement or considering a refund.

7. What Does Not Qualify

A replacement or refund may not be available when the reported issue is caused by circumstances outside the purchased proxy service.

  • Customer-side configuration problems
  • Incompatible software or applications
  • Incorrect use of proxy credentials
  • Restrictions imposed by third-party websites or services
  • Customer use that violates our Terms of Service or Acceptable Use Policy
  • Changes in the customer's requirements after purchase

This list is not exhaustive. Each request may be evaluated according to its specific circumstances.

8. How to Request Support

If you experience a service problem, contact NexProxy support through Telegram:

@NexProxyHQ

When contacting support, please provide enough information for us to identify the order and investigate the reported issue.

9. Verification

NexProxy may perform reasonable checks to determine whether a reported proxy issue is a genuine service failure.

Customers may be asked to provide relevant connection details, error information, timestamps, or other technical information needed to investigate the issue.

Verification requirements may vary depending on the nature of the reported problem.

10. Bulk / Custom Orders

Bulk and custom orders may be subject to specific commercial terms agreed between the customer and NexProxy before purchase.

Where specific refund, replacement, delivery, or support terms have been agreed for a custom order, those terms may apply to that order.

11. Payment Provider Rules

Transactions may be processed through third-party payment providers. Payment-provider rules may impose additional requirements or restrictions on refunds and payment disputes.

Where applicable, NexProxy will follow the relevant requirements of the payment provider used for the transaction.

12. Chargebacks and Payment Disputes

Customers are encouraged to contact NexProxy support first when they experience a service problem so that the issue can be investigated and, where appropriate, resolved through support.

Nothing in this policy limits any rights that a customer may have under applicable law or the rules of the payment provider.

13. No Guarantee of Third-Party Compatibility

Proxy performance can vary depending on the destination website, application, network environment, security controls, and third-party policies.

A proxy being unavailable or restricted by a particular third-party service does not automatically mean that the proxy itself has experienced a service failure.

14. Policy Changes

NexProxy may update this Refund & Replacement Policy when necessary to reflect changes to the service, payment processes, operational practices, or applicable requirements.

The latest version published on this website will apply to future purchases unless otherwise required by applicable law.

15. Contact

For refund, replacement, or service-related support, contact NexProxy through Telegram:

@NexProxyHQ
